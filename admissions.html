Admissions

Admission to Nawaz Sharif School of Eminence, Boys Campus, Haroonabad, is completely free of cost.

100% Free

No Admission Fee, No Tuition Fee

We believe every child deserves quality education regardless of financial background. Admission, tuition and books are provided free of charge to all enrolled students.

Process

Admission Process

Admission Form

Download the latest admission form

Download the official two-page admission form.

Download Form

1. Collect the Admission Form

Available free of cost from the school office.

2. Submit Documents

B-Form/CNIC copy, previous school result card, and 2 passport-size photographs.

3. Entry Test / Interview

A basic assessment or interview appropriate to the class level.

4. Confirmation

Once approved, your child's seat is confirmed at no cost.

5. Welcome to NSSE!

Class placement and orientation begin your child's journey with us.

Required Documents

B-Form, result card, photographs, and a character certificate where applicable.

Admission Timing

Admissions are open year-round, though the April session has limited seats.

Cost

There is no fee at any stage -- admission, tuition and core learning materials are fully free.
